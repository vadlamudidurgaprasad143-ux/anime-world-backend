Tanjiro Kamado

A kind-hearted boy who becomes a Demon Slayer to cure his sister Nezuko and avenge his family.

Nezuko Kamado

Tanjiro's younger sister who was turned into a demon, but fights alongside her brother to protect humans.

Zenitsu Agatsuma

A cowardly Demon Slayer who unlocks his true power of Thunder Breathing when he falls asleep.

Inosuke Hashibira

A wild boy raised by boars who uses Beast Breathing and fights with dual serrated swords.

Kyojuro Rengoku

The cheerful and powerful Flame Hashira with an unwavering moral compass and incredible strength.
