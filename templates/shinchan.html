Shinnosuke Nohara (Shinchan)

A mischievous 5-year-old boy who loves Action Kamen, chocobi snacks, and leads his friends in the Kasukabe Defense Group.

Misae Nohara

Shinchan's short-tempered but loving mother who manages the household, loves discount shopping sales, and keeps Shinchan in line.

Hiroshi Nohara

Shinchan's hard-working father who works at a corporate office, loves drinking beer after work, and suffers from notoriously smelly feet.

Himawari Nohara

Shinchan's baby sister who loves shiny objects, expensive jewelry, and handsome young men.

Shiro

The Nohara family's fluffy white pet dog who is extremely smart, responsible, and often rescues Shinchan from trouble.

Toru Kazama

Shin-chan's smart, polite, and wealthy best friend who attends cram school and gets easily embarrassed by Shin-chan's antics.

Nene Sakurada

A cheerful girl who forces her friends to play realistic house games and secretly vents her anger on a stuffed rabbit doll.

Masao Sato

A timid, gentle, and sensitive boy who cries easily, loves drawing comic books, and is easily frightened by bullies.

Bo-chan

A calm, quiet, and wise boy who rarely speaks, loves collecting unique rocks, and always has a running nose.
