Sung Jin-Woo

Originally known as the "Weakest E-Rank Hunter," he gains the unique ability to level up infinitely after surviving a Double Dungeon, eventually becoming the Shadow Monarch.

Cha Hae-In

A powerful S-Rank Hunter and Vice-Guild Master of the Hunters Guild. She possesses exceptional sword skills and has a unique sense of smell for mana.

Choi Jong-In

An S-Rank Mage Hunter known as "The Ultimate Weapon." He is the Guild Master of the Hunters Guild and specializes in destructive fire magic.

Baek Yoon-Ho

An S-Rank Hunter and Master of the White Tiger Guild. He possesses beast transformation magic, granting him immense physical power and reflexes.

Blood-Red Commander Igris

A noble knight shadow extracted by Jin-Woo. Fiercely loyal and highly skilled with a broadsword, he serves as one of Jin-Woo's strongest army commanders.
