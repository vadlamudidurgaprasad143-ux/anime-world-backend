Doraemon

A robotic cat from the 22nd century. He helps Nobita with various futuristic gadgets from his 4D pocket.

Nobita Nobi

A lazy elementary school student who relies on Doraemon's gadgets to solve his daily problems.

Shizuka Minamoto

A smart, kind, and sweet girl who is Nobita's best friend and love interest.

Takeshi 'Gian' Goda

A strong and tough boy known for his short temper and terrible singing voice, but deeply cares for his friends.

Suneo Honekawa

A wealthy boy who loves showing off his gadgets and toys, often hanging out with Gian.
